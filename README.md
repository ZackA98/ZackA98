### About Me

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Sacramento City College who learns best by building. My background is in hands-on fabrication and design — from leading the design, fabrication, and build department for my high school's First Robotics Competition team, to independently machining parts, welding, and sourcing components for personal projects like a hubless brushless DC motor built to power a new type of stand-up electric scooter, and fabricating custom suspension and drivetrain components for a 1990 Jeep YJ rock crawler build.

I'm drawn to problems that require turning an idea into a physical, working object: sourcing parts, prototyping, testing, and iterating until it actually works.
